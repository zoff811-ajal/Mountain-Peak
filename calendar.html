Still to decide before this becomes a published calendar:

  • Banked closure days are spare capacity: they keep the year at 163 even if you lose days to weather, and are released if you don't.
  • Juneteenth falls on 19 June and can collide with make-up days in the closed-holidays version.
  • Teacher work days, parent conferences, and assessment windows are not counted here — they would sit on flex days or inside an intersession.
  • Check your state's minimum for instructional hours, not just days. At 163 days the daily block has to be long enough to clear it.
  • The alternative to June make-up days is trimming a week from an intersession, which pulls the whole back half of the year forward.
