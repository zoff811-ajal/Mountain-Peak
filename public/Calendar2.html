The 172-day alternating flex calendar

A year-round schedule built on a two-week rotation, sized to Colorado's minimum for an independent school. Every week of the rotation is worth four attendance days, so 172 days falls on exactly 43 instructional weeks.

Compulsory attendance is satisfied where a child is enrolled at an independent, private, or parochial school for a minimum of 172 days (C.R.S. 22-33-104). The home-based education statute sets the same 172 days, averaging four instructional contact hours per day (C.R.S. 22-33-104.5).

Before this becomes a published calendar:

  • 172 is a floor, not a plan. Scheduling exactly 172 means one unplanned closure puts the year out of compliance, so bank a few days or run the 180-day setting.
  • The days requirement travels with an hours expectation. Confirm which standard your accreditor or umbrella school applies before fixing the daily block.
  • Teacher work days, conferences, and assessment windows are not counted here. They belong on flex days or inside an intersession, not on student days.
  • Attendance records have to show the 172 days actually happened, not just that they were scheduled.
  • None of this is legal advice. Read C.R.S. 22-33-104 yourself, or have counsel confirm it, before you publish dates to families.
